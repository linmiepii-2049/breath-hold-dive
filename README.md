# 一口气 · Breath Hold

A freediving underwater-photography game. One dive is one day: optionally train on the beach, swim a coral reef, shoot five frames, and submit them to the Deep Sea Photography Association. Prize money buys fins, a lens, and a wetsuit.

Play the build at [https://linmiepii-2049.github.io/breath-hold-dive/](https://linmiepii-2049.github.io/breath-hold-dive/) after GitHub Pages is set to deploy from GitHub Actions.

界面默认简体中文，右上角可切到 English。

## Play

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the URL Vite prints (`/breath-hold-dive/`). Then:

1. **岸上训练 Shore training** (optional). Two sessions per in-game day. The day advances when you submit a dive, so training cannot be ground forever.
2. **下潜 Dive.** Spend the breath. Deeper water burns oxygen faster. Leave enough to get back up.
3. At the surface, press **E**. The prompt in the meter panel says 按 E 结束下潜, with E drawn as a key cap. There is no on-screen button, because a locked pointer cannot click one. Putting your head on the surface refills oxygen to full at once, so you can breathe and dive again without returning to shore.
4. **提交协会 Submit.** Each frame gets a judge card. A photo under 60 that still has an animal is returned (退回 / Rejected), pays ¥0, and still enters the album. A frame with no animal says it is not added. Accepted frames pay the best one in full, plus 25% of the others, minus any rescue fines.
5. **装备店 Gear shop.** Buy an upgrade, then dive again. The Blue Hole site is only a teaser in v1.

`npm run build` typechecks and writes `dist/`. `npm run check` also runs the scoring assertions.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse | Steer. Up looks up, right turns right. The diver swims the way the camera faces, including up and down. Pitch stays within about ±80°. |
| Space | Fin kick, thrust only forward along the look |
| F | Sprint kick (faster, burns oxygen faster) |
| Right click | Toggle the viewfinder. Steering stays the same; the overlay, wheel zoom, and shutter are added |
| Mouse wheel | Focal length, while the viewfinder is open |
| Left click | Shutter (5 frames), while the viewfinder is open |
| E at the surface | End the dive and keep the photos |
| Esc | Leave the viewfinder, release the mouse, and pause. Click the picture to steer again |
| P | Show or hide the frame-rate counter (off unless you press P or open `?fps=1`) |

On a phone or tablet the keyboard stays unused. The page asks for landscape and, in portrait, covers the game with “请把设备转成横屏”. The left thumb drags the left half of the screen to look, the same way the mouse does (up looks up, right turns right), and that finger is tracked on its own so a right-hand button does not cancel it. 踢水, 冲刺, 相机, and 快门 sit under the right thumb. 踢水 is a hold. 快门 appears only while the viewfinder is open. 结束下潜 replaces E and shows only at the surface. A pause button sits in the top corner and does not eat a left-side drag, and neither does the oxygen bar. A faint hint on the left half fades after the first few seconds of a dive. Shore, training, the album, the shop, and the settlement take taps. The apnea drill is a hold button. Every control sentence on those screens uses that wording (左半边滑动转向, 右手按住踢水, 冲刺, 相机, 快门, 结束下潜), in Chinese and English. A desktop mouse and keyboard are unchanged, and the desktop sentences stay on mouse, Space, F, and E.

Look sensitivity defaults to 1 and can be dragged from 0.45 to 2.1 on the shore. Above about 12 m the diver floats when the fins are still, up to about 0.8 m/s. Around 12 m is neutral. Below that the diver sinks slowly, and only reaches about 0.9 m/s well below 12 m. A Space tap is one fin stroke and coasts about 0.6–0.8 m. Holding Space strokes every 0.45 s and is up to about 2.45 m/s within a second, then letting go glides for about a second before the drift takes over. F is a one-second sprint at about 1.7× that speed. Straight ahead of the spawn the bottom drops past 12 m. A head at the surface refills oxygen instantly and the dive continues until E, a blackout, or a forced ascent. If the mouse unlocks, the dive pauses on a full-screen “click to steer” prompt.

Being hit by a shark or an orca, or running out of oxygen, forces you up and costs stamina and prize money. Photos already shot are kept.

## What is in the water

Reef Wall only. Eight animals: sardine school, clownfish (by the anemone), parrotfish, sea turtle, manta, octopus (changes color), shark, orca (deep only).

Three moments: a shark scattering the bait ball, the turtle surfacing to breathe, and an orca passing through the trench. The field guide in the album says where to look. Each species and each moment keeps your best-scoring thumbnail in `localStorage` (`breath-hold-dive-v1`). A new photo replaces that tile when it ties or beats the stored score, so an older thumbnail does not stick forever. Money, gear, and which slots you have already filled carry over from older saves.

## Decisions

Numbers below are the ones the build actually uses.

- **Camera and swim.** The mouse steers yaw and pitch whenever the pointer is locked, viewfinder open or closed. Mouse up looks up. Mouse right turns right (a rightward move decreases yaw, because positive yaw looks toward screen-left). Pitch is clamped to ±80°. Space and F thrust only along that look. There is no WASD, no strafe, and no separate descend key: look down and kick to go down, look up and kick to go up. Speed bleeds off with drag 0.65 per second (about a 1.1 s half-life), so letting go of Space glides before buoyancy takes over. The diver is prone along the look, with a fin kick while thrusting and a quiet glide otherwise. The third-person camera follows yaw only and holds one elevated rear three-quarter at every depth: 3.0 m behind, 1.7 m to the side (about 30° off the tail), and 3.45 m up, which is a 45° elevation. It looks at a point 0.65 m above the feet, on the torso. If that rear spot is inside the terrain, the camera pulls closer along the same 45° ray instead of dropping down onto the sand. Fins are about 0.28 m wide and 0.9 m long, and the kick lifts them toward the camera. Each reef rock and wall rock has an oriented box in the mesh's own rotation, sized to the scaled icosahedron so a ledge or a tall wall contains the stone. The push uses a capsule from the shoulders through the fins, plus spheres on the head and arms, and keeps about 0.3 m of clearance outside each rock. Terrain contact resolves along the slope normal and never adds upward speed, so a descent does not launch the diver back up the sand. On the sand the diver slides out sideways. The mouse-look clamp is a rate (80 px and 60 px per 1/60 s), so a turn covers the same angle per second at 15 FPS as at 60. Large animals push too; a turtle does not deal damage. Right click only toggles the viewfinder overlay. Esc releases the mouse and pauses; click to lock it again. Default look sensitivity is 1 (0.00115 rad/px yaw, 0.00095 rad/px pitch), adjustable on the shore from 0.45 to 2.1 and stored on the existing save.
- **Breath.** Drain is `0.82 + 0.04 × depth` oxygen per second, and it pauses shallower than 1.25 m. Arriving in the surface band (depth under 1.05 m, or the head at the surface) refills oxygen to full immediately, including after a blackout. Base pool is 100, before lung training. A wetsuit cuts drain 9% per level. Apnea training cuts it up to 15% more. Oxygen at zero is a blackout: −50 stamina, a ¥70 rescue fine, and a locked ascent. The dive ends once that ascent has held the surface, and the photos already taken are kept. If the ascent makes no progress for 4 s, or 18 s pass and the diver is still underwater, the rescue ends the dive the same way.
- **Buoyancy.** Neutral at 12 m. Shallower, upward acceleration is `0.47 × (12 − depth) / 12` m/s², so a still diver floats at about 0.72 m/s near the surface. Deeper, downward acceleration ramps to 0.585 m/s² over the next 24 m (full only around 36 m, about 0.3 m/s at 20 m) and the free-fall settles near 0.9 m/s. Drag stays 0.65 per second. Reaching the surface refills oxygen and does not end the dive; E does, and so does a blackout or a forced ascent once it has held them there.
- **Kick.** Space fires a fin stroke immediately (0.98 m/s along the look, before fins) and another every 0.45 s while held. After 0.12 s the hold settles on a 2.45 m/s cruise. A tap that ends before that is just the one stroke and coasts about 0.6–0.8 m. F snaps to 4.16 m/s (1.7×) for 1.0 s and multiplies oxygen drain by 7.5. The 3.4 s cooldown starts when that second ends, and the HUD shows the full 3.4 then, not a clock that was already running during the sprint. Both speeds scale with `1 + 0.14 × fin level`. The fins sweep together through about 60° on that same clock, with a small body undulation. Fish, sharks, turtles, and rays wag a tail or flap fins on top of the swim.
- **Film and money.** Five shots. A score under 60 that still has a subject is returned: the card and the settlement say 退回 / Rejected, the prize is ¥0, and the photo still enters the album. A frame with no animal is an empty shot: the contact sheet says 空镜 · 不入图鉴 / Empty frame · not in the album, and it is not stored. Five frames sit on one row at 1280px wide. At 60 and above, prize for a score `t` (0–1) is `round(36t + 300t²)` in-game ¥. A 60 pays ¥130; a 100 pays ¥336. Payout is the best accepted frame plus 25% of the rest. A hit costs ¥45 and a blackout costs ¥70, taken from the balance even when this dive earned less. The balance cannot fall below ¥0.
- **Score, out of 100.** The four lines on the judge card are integers, and the total is their sum. Composition 30 (rule of thirds 12, size in frame 10, fully inside 8). Rarity up to 25: sardine 10, the school treated as one subject 14, clownfish 12, parrotfish 13, turtle 16, manta 18, shark 20, octopus 18 while camouflaged and 25 while it is showing off, orca 25. The moment line has a base of 10 for a clear, framed shot of a creature that is actually moving (a stroke, a turn, feeding, a color show). A posed or hovering animal does not get that base. A readable subject that is already well placed is scored at the top of the composition line, so a clear moving clownfish lands in the low 60s while the same frame sitting still, or a small off-center one, stays under 60. The card labels that 动作中 +10 / In motion +10. The three special moments still score on top of that, up to 25, and the card says 特殊瞬间 / Special moment. A special moment scores only while that moment is actually happening and its animal is plainly in frame: center inside the picture, body at least 18% of the frame height (24% for an orca), and not hidden behind reef or lost in haze. That is 25 points when it also fills the frame and is composed, otherwise 12. A clear photo of a common animal in action lands around 60–70. A sloppy or idle frame stays under 60. Weed, rock, or fog in front of the subject cuts composition and rarity. A dark speck that is not a readable orca does not get rarity 25 or the pass. Distance and danger share 20. The obvious subject in the frame wins over a rarer speck. The payout screen is titled 结算 / Settlement and lists the best frame's score next to the money.
- **Training.** Breathing rhythm is about 30 s including a short unscored lead-in. The running lung reward is on the timer, and the result screen says 训练完成 with the gain. Each beat the white dot crosses a bright band (the middle-to-late half of the beat, 50%–88%). Tap the matching key while the dot is in the band. An earlier tap does not fail the beat. Holding the right key into the band counts as a good hit; the center of the band (64%–78%) is a perfect. A perfect run is +8% lung, capped at +30%. Static apnea is about 42 s; a perfect hold is +5% efficiency, capped at +15%. Two sessions total per day.
- **Frame rate.** Movement, oxygen, buoyancy, the sprint clock, and shark timers step at a fixed 1/60 s. Each frame adds real elapsed time and catches up by at most 0.25 s, so a 20 FPS machine is not in slow motion. Mouse look and the camera ease once per frame. Press **P**, or load the page with `?fps=1`, to show a counter. It stays off in normal play. F is still the kick. Photo thumbnails are read back through a pixel-buffer so the shutter does not stall the GPU on the spot. The drawing resolution is capped at a device pixel ratio of 1.5, including on touch screens. If the counter stays under about 40, the resolution steps down, but it never goes below 0.6 of the CSS size or a 640px-wide buffer. `?quality=low` locks that floor and the cheap tier. The low tier hides the light shafts and the contact shadow, keeps only a few bubbles, draws half the kelp, and uses a thinner fog. It steps back up only after the rate holds above about 55.
- **Moments.** The bait-ball, turtle-breath, and orca-pass lines appear only while that moment is happening and you are close enough to see it. They do not announce a distant event at the start of a dive. The turtle holds at the surface for about 10 s, just in front of the diver, and the prompt stays up for that whole breath and names the direction. The octopus comes out of the crevice for about 6 s while it changes colour. Those toasts name the direction and keep updating it from the camera, so a turtle in the top-right of the frame reads 上方 · 右前方 rather than a direction from when the toast first appeared. The viewfinder draws a focus bracket about a third of the frame. Each frame projects the animals once, in normalized screen space, and that same result is what the label, the highlight, and the shutter use. An animal counts only when its projected center is inside the bracket (a body that is almost entirely inside still counts if the center is a hair outside). The drawing-buffer scale does not move the bracket. A sardine school does not count just because the cloud covers the reticle. A moving school, judged by the centroid speed or the fastest fish, gets the same +10 motion line as any other moving animal. The bait ball is still the 25-point moment. A breathing turtle inside the bracket scores 海龟换气, including at the distance where the prompt appears.
- **Debug staging.** `?debug=1` adds a closed Debug disclosure on the shore and during a dive. It is absent otherwise. Each button puts you in front of one species or one special moment, aims the viewfinder, refills oxygen, and freezes that scene for about 8 seconds, or until the first photo, so it can be photographed without waiting. `?debug=1` also logs shark state changes (`[shark] patrol -> warn`) and the first push against a rock or a large animal. Two extra buttons do not change the dive numbers: 氧气 3% sets the tank to about 3% so a blackout is a few seconds away, and 鲨鱼警告 starts the reef shark's normal warning ring at 8 m. `window.__bhDebug.warn(meters)` uses that distance as the ring (clamped to the 6.2–11.2 m ring the shark already uses) and `__bhDebug.air()` is the same oxygen drop. They exist only with `?debug=1`. The click that opens that view does not immediately toggle it off. 虎鲸 and 虎鲸过境 frame the orca side-on in open water. 鲨鱼 is a three-quarter side view. 小丑鱼 sits in open water. 章鱼 is on open sand, a little higher in the frame, and cycles its colors. Left click shoots. Right click or Esc leaves the viewfinder.
- **Shop.** Long fins ¥60 / ¥160 / ¥380, +14% kick speed per level. Lens ¥90 / ¥200 / ¥420, viewfinder FOV 34–62°, then 26–72°, 20–82°, 15–90°. Wetsuit ¥110 / ¥240 / ¥500.
- **Danger.** Two sharks. For the first 20 s the reef shark is parked far from spawn and its mesh is hidden, so it is not a viewfinder subject near the start. It does not aggro during that time either. The reef shark patrols about 3–19 m: a midwater pass near 3 m in open water, then the wall, which the deeper sand now carries to about 19 m. The trench shark patrols the deep pocket (about x 4–24, z 50–76) from roughly 27 m down to the floor near 41 m, so a diver at 30 m or 36 m in that pocket is on the route. They do not hunt the whole map. A diver floating at about 3 m above the reef shark's route, rather than on that midwater pass, is outside the patrol on purpose and the shark does not come up to meet them. Inside about 11 m a shark turns on a fixed circle for about 1 s (the HUD says 鲨鱼正在靠近, and a toast says 鲨鱼转过来了). That circle does not follow you. A sprint, or a hard turn, during the warning or the charge makes the shark give up. Drifting without either does not. The sprint has to hold for a moment: one frame of being pushed off a rock is not a sprint. A diver who only sinks, rises, or drifts is followed until the shark makes contact, at any depth the patrol actually swims, including the reef around 12 m and the trench pocket around 35–40 m. The charge cue is separate: the HUD and a toast say 冲过来了！ and the screen edge tints red, lightly enough that the reef stays readable. The charge homes on where the diver is now, closes horizontally at 3.6 m/s, under the 4.16 m/s sprint, and counts a hit within about 3.5 m when the diver is not sprinting or turning hard. It lasts up to 12 s. A hit costs 36 stamina and shoves you upward. After that the shark waits about 12 s. The orca only crosses the deep trench, and only now and then. Stamina starts at 100; a blackout costs 50 and locks the ascent. A blackout fine comes off the balance and the settlement shows 实发 as ¥0 when the fine is larger than the prize. Shore training grants lung and oxygen-efficiency only when the drill finishes; leaving early grants nothing. Pausing at the surface still accepts E to end the dive; underwater the pause line does not offer it. Above 12 m the water lifts you; through the top 6 m that passive rise settles near 0.8 m/s, and a kick downward still outruns it.
- **Bottom.** Straight ahead of the spawn (around x = 6, heading +Z) the sand falls away within a few metres, so a straight descent does not meet the floor at 8 m. The offshore lane keeps dropping past 23 m. Warm gold beads, drawn without fog and joined by a trail, sit just beside the descent and fade out within about 2.5 m of the diver so the trail does not cut through the body. They lead into the trench, where the floor keeps falling to about 41 m. An orange branch of the same markers runs from spawn to the clownfish ledge. Nearby reef within about 10 m gets extra fill and a rim so it does not read as a silhouette, and the fog near the diver is thinner on the low tier than on the default tier. A shark warning or charge pulses a red vignette over the whole frame while the center stays readable. The viewfinder label says 已对准 when a subject is in the bracket. Any moving creature earns the motion line, however far it is. A creature whose body covers the center of the bracket is the subject even when its own center sits just outside. After an underwater blackout the settlement names the stamina cut and the fine. An empty shutter is not added to the album. A kick down that line reaches the trench channel, about 33 m and deeper, inside one breath with oxygen left. The sand does not push the diver back up the slope. The shelf to the left of that heading stays shallow. The clownfish anemone sits on that shelf, a little deeper than the 6–7 m you arrive at, so a slight look down puts the fish in the lower part of the view. They keep circling.
- **Look.** Shallow fog `#178f9c`, deep fog `#143246`, above-water haze `#b7e0ec`. Fog density stays low enough that the water within about 8 m does not disappear, on the full tier and the low tier. The sand under the diver is lifted back up inside that 8 m. A fill light and a light at the diver's chest ride with you and do not fade with depth. The diver is not fogged. A teal emissive edge is already on at the surface, so the suit stays readable after a blackout, and it strengthens through the deep so the suit is not a flat silhouette below 30 m. The sand keeps its colour down to the 41 m floor. UI accent `#8ee0d6`. Pixel ratio is capped at 1.5 and drops further when the frame rate stays under about 40. Large animals are judged on the body in frame, not nose-to-tail. The body is held about 0.3 m clear of rock, so the suit does not sit inside a stone. If the browser cannot create a WebGL context, the page shows 你的浏览器无法启动 3D instead of a blank screen.
- **Site layout** is seeded (2049) so the anemone, the octopus crevice, and the trench stay put.

## Assets

No downloaded models, textures, or audio files. Terrain, animals, the diver, caustics, and light shafts are built from Three.js primitives. Ambience, heartbeat, shutter, bubbles, and shore wash are synthesized with WebAudio. Code and these generated visuals are original to this repository.

## Deploy

`.github/workflows/pages.yml` builds on every push to `main` and deploys with GitHub Pages. In the repo settings, set Pages → Build and deployment → Source to **GitHub Actions** once. Vite's `base` is `/breath-hold-dive/`.
